## Full description

Build a calmer, more intentional study rhythm with FocusForge. Start a session, follow your own study and rest cycle, and keep your study plan, notes, and progress together on your device.

FocusForge includes a configurable study timer with clear round and rest cues, weekly focus goals, subjects, session goals, and completion tracking. The History workspace turns recent study activity into practical charts, subject summaries, focus feedback, and goal progress.

Plan ahead with the seven-day routine planner. Add hour-by-hour blocks, then start a Focus session directly from a routine block when it is time to begin. The Notes workspace supports private notes, checklists, custom folders, and reusable tags.

FocusForge also includes Android focus guidance for local cues, Do Not Disturb, Screen Pinning, and emergency-interruption setup. Android owns system-wide interruption and app-switching controls; FocusForge explains the available device settings rather than promising restrictions a regular Android app cannot enforce.

### Advertising choice

FocusForge may show a small banner on the Focus dashboard. You may optionally watch a rewarded ad to receive 12 hours without banners. The timer, notes, routine planner, history, and all core study tools remain available without watching a rewarded ad.

### Designed around privacy

FocusForge is local-first. Your sessions, notes, routines, goals, and manually entered emergency-app labels stay on your device. No account or cloud sync is required. Advertising consent and ad delivery are handled through the advertising SDK; no study records are sent to it.
