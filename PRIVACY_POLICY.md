# FocusForge Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 23 August 2026

FocusForge is a private study-focus timer, routine planner, and notes workspace. This policy explains how the released Android application handles information.

## Local-first design

FocusForge does not require an account. Study sessions, timer settings, goals, subject tags, session outcomes, notes, folders, tags, weekly routines, and manually entered emergency-app labels are stored locally on the user’s own device. FocusForge does not provide cloud sync, a developer-operated database, or sale of user information.

FocusForge does not transmit these local study, note, goal, or routine records to the developer. The app may display a compact banner ad on the Focus dashboard and offers an optional rewarded ad that grants twelve hours without ads after the advertising network reports a completed reward. The advertising SDK may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, and consent choices as described in Google’s own policies. FocusForge does not send the user’s study records to the advertising SDK.

## Advertising and choice

Before requesting ads, FocusForge uses Google’s consent flow where required by applicable law and the advertising configuration. Users can use all core study features without viewing a rewarded ad. A rewarded ad is voluntary and only controls the locally stored twelve-hour ad-free period; it never unlocks or restricts timer, notes, history, or routine-planning features. The current development build uses Google test ads, which do not generate revenue. A future production release will use the developer’s own AdMob inventory and will update its Google Play Data safety answers to match the final SDK configuration.

## Android controls

FocusForge can request Android notification permission to deliver local timer cues. It can also open Android’s Do Not Disturb access settings when the user chooses to use Priority DND. Android controls the final notification-channel, battery, Do Not Disturb, and emergency-exception behavior. FocusForge does not read a public-release user’s full installed-app inventory; users may type personal emergency-app labels into the local setup checklist.

## Security and deletion

FocusForge stores its local information in the application’s sandboxed storage. Removing application data or uninstalling FocusForge removes locally stored FocusForge records from the device, subject to any device-level backup settings controlled by the user and Android. Advertising-consent choices and advertising SDK data are governed by the relevant platform and Google policies rather than FocusForge’s local study storage.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email displayed on the FocusForge Google Play listing.

## Policy changes

Material privacy changes will be described in an updated version of this policy before the related app release is made available.
